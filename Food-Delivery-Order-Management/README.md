# Food Delivery & Order Management System

## Project Overview

This is a self-initiated Business Analysis portfolio project created to demonstrate practical Business Analysis skills including requirement analysis, stakeholder identification, process mapping, requirements documentation, user stories, acceptance criteria and UAT.

**Project Type:** Self-Initiated Portfolio Project  
**Role:** Business Analyst  
**Methodology:** Agile / Scrum  
**Tools:** Jira, Confluence, Draw.io, Figma, SQL, Power BI, Excel and AI Tools

## Business Problem

Customers may face difficulties when ordering food through traditional methods such as phone calls or direct restaurant visits. Restaurants may also manage orders manually, which can result in order errors, delays and limited visibility of order status.

The proposed system provides a centralized platform where customers can search restaurants, view menus, place orders, make payments and track deliveries.

## Business Objective

The objective is to design a food delivery and order management system that:

- Simplifies the food ordering process
- Improves order visibility and tracking
- Reduces manual order management
- Helps restaurants manage customer orders efficiently
- Provides customers with a convenient digital ordering experience
