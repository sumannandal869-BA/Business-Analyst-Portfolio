# Process Analysis

## 1. AS-IS Process

The current/traditional food ordering process can involve manual communication between the customer and restaurant.

### AS-IS Flow

Customer
↓
Calls / visits restaurant
↓
Checks menu and availability
↓
Places order
↓
Restaurant manually records order
↓
Restaurant prepares food
↓
Customer arranges / waits for delivery
↓
Order delivered

---

## 2. AS-IS Pain Points

The current process may have the following challenges:

- Customers may need to contact restaurants manually.
- Menu availability may not be visible in real time.
- Orders may be recorded manually.
- Manual order entry can result in incorrect orders.
- Customers have limited visibility of order status.
- Payment and order information may not be centralized.
- Customers may need to contact the restaurant for updates.
- There may be limited centralized order history.

---

## 3. TO-BE Process

The proposed system digitizes the food ordering process.

### TO-BE Flow

Customer
↓
Login / Register
↓
Search Restaurant
↓
View Menu
↓
Add Items to Cart
↓
Checkout
↓
Enter Delivery Address
↓
Make Payment
↓
Order Confirmation
↓
Restaurant Accepts Order
↓
Restaurant Prepares Food
↓
Delivery Partner Picks Up Order
↓
Customer Tracks Delivery
↓
Order Delivered
↓
Customer Reviews / Rates Order

---

## 4. Expected Improvements

The proposed system is expected to:

- Reduce manual order processing.
- Improve order accuracy.
- Provide better order visibility.
- Simplify customer ordering.
- Improve communication through notifications.
- Provide centralized order history.
- Improve restaurant order management.
- Provide customers with convenient online payment.

---

## 5. Gap Analysis

| Area | AS-IS | TO-BE | Gap / Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant Search | Manual search/calls | Digital search | Centralized restaurant discovery |
| Menu | Customer asks restaurant | Digital menu | Online menu visibility |
| Order Entry | Manual | Digital | Reduce manual errors |
| Payment | Manual / separate process | Online payment | Integrated payment |
| Order Tracking | Limited | Digital tracking | Improved visibility |
| Notifications | Manual communication | System notifications | Faster communication |
| Order History | Limited | Centralized history | Better customer experience |
| Reviews | Informal feedback | Digital ratings/reviews | Structured feedback |

---

## 6. Business Value

The proposed solution can provide value to both customers and restaurants by digitizing the ordering process, improving visibility, reducing manual activities and creating a more structured order management experience.
